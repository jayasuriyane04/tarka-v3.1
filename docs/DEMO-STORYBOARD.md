Recording owner: user. Planned duration: approximately three minutes. Use an adult-owned dummy profile; keep PIN and credentials off camera. This is a scripted demonstration, not a student study.

| Time | Action | Explain |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:25 | Show adult setup with age band and separate voice/AI opt-ins | Thirty lessons across maths/reading; V3 extends V2; a parent sets the pace |
| 0:25–0:55 | Open a lesson, choose an installed English voice and pace, press Listen, pause and resume | Device speech reads permitted teaching text; no microphone and no automatic playback |

| 1:25–1:55 | Reach the new transfer item, listen and answer | Narration on transfer counts as support; text and transcript remain visible |
| 1:55–2:20 | Reload, unlock parent space, inspect narration/hint counts and export | Saved progress distinguishes completion from independent transfer evidence |
| 2:20–2:40 | Show one newly added reading lesson and its distinct transfer passage | Twelve new lessons extend the eighteen original lessons; no full-curriculum claim |
| 2:40–3:00 | Show STATUS and explain provider boundaries | Optional NVIDIA hint selection via Nebius is separate from device audio; cite only verified results |

Rehearse real device audio first. If no local voice is available, show the truthful text fallback and do not dub a synthetic voice over it as if the application produced it. Record a real NVIDIA segment only after account access and budgeted synthetic inference have been verified; otherwise say live AI is pending. Show failures/fallbacks honestly. No child participation or learning improvement is claimed by the demo.
